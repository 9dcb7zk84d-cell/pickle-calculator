Bread & Butter pickles [Cornersmith]

Your batch

Food-grade calcium chloride flakes (77% CaCl₂, E509). Dose: ⅛ Australian tsp per litre of brine.

Ingredients

IngredientYour batch

The source gives no vinegar acidity. This calculator uses 5% as its baseline. Choose food-grade vinegar labelled with the selected acidity. Australian measures: 1 tbsp = 20 mL and 1 tsp = 5 mL.

Method

  1. Slice the cucumbers into thin rounds. Toss with the salting portion and refrigerate for 1–2 hours or overnight. Drain well.
  2. In a non-reactive saucepan, combine the vinegar, water, caster sugar, turmeric and brine salt. Stir to dissolve the sugar, then bring to a boil. If using calcium chloride, dissolve the measured flakes in the brine.
  3. Toss the drained cucumbers with the thinly sliced onions, mustard, fennel, dill and optional chilli.
  4. Pack loosely into clean, heatproof jars. Divide the peppercorns between the jars, then pour in hot brine to cover. Release trapped air, leave about 1 cm headspace and fit the lids.
  5. Heat-process the jars.

Jar preparation and processing

Sterilising empty jars

Miele recommends using Disinfect items or placing empty jars upside down and steaming at 100 °C for 10 minutes.

Heat processing filled jars

In the original book recipe, Cornersmith specifies 10 minutes for its approximately 375 mL jars. Page 180 describes a stovetop water bath. Stand the jars upright in a deep stockpot, spaced apart and clear of the sides. Start with water close to the jars’ temperature to reduce thermal shock, cover the jars with water, and start timing once the water boils. The 10-minute timing is reported for the original recipe and jar size.

Miele steam oven

For its own pickled cucumber recipe, Miele specifies Steam at 75 °C for 40 minutes for filled, sealed jars of the same size. Miele advises leaving the jars in the switched-off oven for a further 30 minutes, then cooling for about 24 hours. Miele Australia, Pickled cucumbers.

Use Miele’s steam schedule with the linked Miele recipe. For a refrigerated batch from this calculator, refrigerate promptly at 4 °C or colder.
