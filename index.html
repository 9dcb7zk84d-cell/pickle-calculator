Sterilising Jars

Miele DGC 5080 XL · Steam Cooking / Universal · 100 °C · 15 minutes

  1. Check and wash. Use heatproof preserving jars with sound rims and no cracks or chips. Remove lids and seals. Wash the jars in hot soapy water and rinse thoroughly. Steam lids and seals only if their manufacturer confirms they can withstand steam at 100 °C. Otherwise, prepare them according to their instructions.
  2. Load the tray. Put the empty jars on their sides or mouth down in a perforated steam tray. Space jars and any compatible loose parts so they do not touch. Slide the tray onto the lowest shelf level and fill the oven’s water container.
  3. Run the cycle. From the oven’s main menu, select Steam Cooking, then Universal (shown as Cook universal on some displays). Set 100 °C and a 15-minute cooking duration. Let the cycle finish.
  4. Get ready to fill. Remove the tray carefully using oven gloves. Drain the jars and let any steamed lids and seals air-dry on a clean rack. Keep jars warm for hot packing. For cold packing, let them cool as directed by the recipe. Avoid touching the jar interiors and sealing surfaces.

This cycle disinfects empty jars before filling. For shelf-stable batches, follow the tested recipe’s requirements for jar preparation and filled-jar processing.

Adapted from Alex Elliott-Howery’s Pocket Pickler, p. 181, and the Miele DGC 5080 XL operating instructions.
