Method

  1. Wash cucumbers; remove 1.5 mm from the blossom end, leaving about 6 mm of stem at the other end.
  2. Dissolve soak salt in soak water, submerge cucumbers for 12 hours, then drain.
  3. Combine 5%-acidity vinegar, brine water, brine salt and sugar. Tie mixed pickling spice in clean muslin, add and bring to the boil.
  4. Pack cucumbers into preserving jars, distributing mustard seed and dill.
  5. If using calcium chloride, dissolve the measured food-grade product in the brine. For shelf-stable canning, use the product label dose rather than experimental percentage settings.
  6. Cover with boiling solution, leaving 13 mm headspace. Remove bubbles, wipe rims and close jars appropriately.
  7. For shelf-stable storage, follow the full original NCHFP processing instructions and use validated jar sizes and processing times.
